- **Purpose**: Enumerate everything workstream 002 itself owns.

### Statement

The "in" boundary is the complete list of what 002 is responsible for building: the book and its instruments, the full rest/match/cancel/replace lifecycle, the escrow port, the three event types, read-only depth and the halt switch, time-in-force and capacity enforcement, the conservation check over a fill set, snapshot rows for persistence, and draining the inbound ring. Everything in this list is 002's to build even where the real implementation has not yet built it (depth, halt, TIF, caps, snapshot, and ring drain are all still open — see their respective `hard_problem/` and `crate/` entries).
