# Phase: P10 — Best bid/ask

### Scope

- **Purpose**: Prove the book tracks best bid and best ask after inserts.
- **Responsibility**: `book_best_bid`/`book_best_ask` reflect the current top of each side.

**Design status**: Proven by `demo_p10_best` (`module/smoke_exchange_phases/src/bin/demo_p10_best.rs`), but not through functions of those two names — no separate `book_best_bid`/`book_best_ask` exist. The real API is one method, `Book::best(instrument, side)`, taking the side as a parameter rather than splitting into a pair of side-fixed functions; `exchange_book`'s own module doc explains why (one method covers both sides without duplicating the lookup logic). The demo proves the same fact the phase cares about — best bid and best ask are both correct after two inserts, without a full scan — by calling `best` twice, once per side.

### Statement

The tenth phase's one new contract: after two inserts, the book correctly reports best bid and best ask without a full scan.
