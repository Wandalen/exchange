# Phase: P17 — Conservation assertion

### Scope

- **Purpose**: Prove the conservation check distinguishes an ordinary batch from one it must refuse.
- **Responsibility**: `conserve_assert` passes on an ordinary batch, fails on one carrying an inexact notional.

### Statement

The seventeenth phase's one new contract: a batch of ordinary trades always conserves — each trade's own debit and credit are the same number by construction — while a trade whose notional cannot be expressed exactly is refused rather than silently approximated.

Revised from the original "10 and -10 sums to zero, 10 and -9 does not" framing, which assumed two trades in a batch could fail to offset each other. Once `conserve_assert` pushes both of a trade's legs (debit and credit) rather than one signed leg, that is no longer constructible — see `exchange_conserve/src/lib.rs`'s "Revision" section for why, and why `ConserveError::Unbalanced` is consequently unreachable through this function today.
