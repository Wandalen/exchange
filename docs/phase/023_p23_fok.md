# Phase: P23 — FOK on a thin book

### Scope

- **Purpose**: Prove FOK is genuinely all-or-nothing, with zero side effects on rejection.
- **Responsibility**: A FOK order that cannot fully fill is rejected and the book is left unchanged.

### Statement

The twenty-third phase's one new contract: submitting a FOK order against a book too thin to fill it completely produces a reject, and the book afterward is identical to the book before — no partial fill leaks through.
