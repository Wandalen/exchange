# Tif: Ioc

### Scope

- **Purpose**: Let an order take whatever it can immediately and discard the rest instead of resting.
- **Responsibility**: One of the three time-in-force values the source design names.
- **In Scope**: Suppressing the rest step after a partial or zero fill.
- **Out of Scope**: The matching logic that produces the partial fill itself.

**Design status**: Built as named — `exchange_tif::Tif::Ioc` (`src/lib.rs`).
`tif_rests( Tif::Ioc )` is `false`; `exchange_core`'s `step_place` and
`exchange_inbound::inbound_apply` both consult it and drop the remainder.
`exchange_match::cross` does not — dropping a remainder is the caller's step,
not the crossing loop's.

### Statement

"Immediate Or Cancel" fills whatever quantity is available against the opposite ladder right now and drops the remainder rather than resting it.
